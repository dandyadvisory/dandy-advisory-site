Investor Readiness

Clarify the opportunity before capital engagement.

The Investor Readiness Diagnostic assesses whether an enterprise or project is sufficiently structured, evidenced and defensible for credible engagement with serious private or institutional capital.

It is an assessment of readiness, not a promise of capital access or investor introductions.

What the Diagnostic Assesses

Decision-grade scrutiny before outreach.

Investment Case

A defensible commercial rationale for external capital.

Structure

Capital requirement, use of funds and transaction logic.

Evidence

Financial, commercial and operating evidence behind the proposition.

Governance

Ownership, authority, management and diligence readiness.

Capital Fit

Realistic forms and sources of capital for the opportunity.

Defensibility

The ability to defend the case under serious scrutiny.
