Outcome

Engagement Fit

Who It Is For

Designed for material capital requirements.

Investor Readiness is designed for enterprises and projects with a real underlying commercial proposition and a genuine requirement for external capital.

Typical engagements include:

  • operating companies pursuing growth or expansion capital;
  • developed projects preparing for financing or investment;
  • asset owners and project sponsors seeking material external capital;
  • management teams seeking independent scrutiny before investor engagement.

It is not designed for concept-stage funding searches or informal requests for investor introductions.

Qualification Before Engagement

How the Process Works

A controlled path from enquiry to decision.

01 — Initial Qualification

Submit essential information about the enterprise or project, authority, capital requirement and current state of readiness.

02 — dandy Advisory Review

The submission is assessed for maturity, authority, commercial credibility and engagement fit.

03 — Investor Readiness Diagnostic

Qualified applicants may be invited to proceed with a fixed-scope professional diagnostic.

04 — Recommendation & Next Path

The assessment concludes with one of four outcomes:

  • Proceed — sufficiently credible and close to engagement-ready.
  • Remediate — viable, but material deficiencies require correction.
  • Pause — fundamental issues remain unresolved.
  • Decline — integrity, authority, legality or fit concerns prevent progression.

Submission does not constitute acceptance of an engagement or investor access.

Decision-Grade Output

What the Diagnostic Produces

Clarity before further expenditure or outreach.

The Investor Readiness Diagnostic provides:

Investor Readiness Scorecard

Provides a structured view of current readiness across the core areas assessed in the diagnostic.

Strengths and Probable Rejection Risks

Identifies the strongest elements of the proposition and the issues most likely to weaken investor confidence.

Capital and Structure Observations

Highlights whether the capital requirement, use of funds, and proposed transaction structure are sufficiently clear and credible.

Documentation and Diligence-Gap Analysis

Shows where supporting evidence, governance information, or diligence materials are incomplete, unclear, or likely to attract scrutiny.

Prioritized Corrective Action Plan

Sets out the most important actions required to strengthen the opportunity before further investor engagement.

Executive Debrief

Summarizes the findings, implications, and recommended next path in a focused management-level discussion.
