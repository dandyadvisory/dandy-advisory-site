Controlled Access

Not an open marketplace.

dandy Advisory does not publicly circulate unverified transactions or provide unrestricted access to commercial relationships. Opportunities are considered only where authority is sufficiently documented, transaction parameters are defined, relevant compliance requirements are understood, and the opportunity remains current.

For qualified buyers and end-users, this may include assessing a defined requirement and, where appropriate, facilitating introductions to relevant sellers, titleholders or mandates. For titleholders, sellers and authorized mandates, it may include assessing whether controlled buyer-side engagement or representation is appropriate.

Interest alone does not establish access. A credible commercial requirement, a defined sell-side position or an authorized mandate is the starting point.

Commercial Scope

Defined requirements. Controlled engagement.

Buyer Requirements & Supply-Side Access

For qualified buyers, end-users and authorized procurement principals, dandy Advisory may assess identified requirements for soft or hard commodities and, where appropriate, facilitate introductions to relevant supply-side principals, sellers or mandates.

The channel is intended for defined requirements — not speculative sourcing requests or general market enquiries.

Sell-Side & Titleholder Representation

For titleholders, sellers and properly authorized mandates, dandy Advisory may consider controlled representation or buyer-side engagement where the product, authority, documentation, commercial parameters and execution pathway are sufficiently clear.

Representation is considered selectively and does not arise merely from the submission of an offer.

Structured Cross-Border Transactions

dandy Advisory may consider defined commercial transactions that require disciplined counterparty qualification, market positioning, documentation, sequencing and execution coordination across jurisdictions.

This may include transactions where the commercial opportunity is credible but the appropriate counterparty pathway must first be established.

Private Structured Situations

Certain private, mandate-based situations outside ordinary commodity trade may be considered on a strictly transaction-specific basis. They are not publicly described, marketed or circulated through this website and remain subject to authority, legal permissibility, counterparty qualification and direct assessment.

The channel is not limited to a fixed public catalogue. Each matter is assessed according to the commodity or transaction, authority, market or jurisdiction, counterparty fit and current availability.

Live opportunities are not publicly listed by default.

Qualification Standard

Defined opportunities require qualified counterparties.

The relevant qualification standard depends on whether you are seeking supply, presenting a sell-side opportunity, acting under mandate or enquiring about a defined structured transaction.

A suitable counterparty should generally demonstrate:

Authority

A clear principal, titleholder, mandate or decision-making role.

Capability

Commercial, operational or financial capacity appropriate to the proposed transaction.

Transaction Fit

The ability to work within the required commodity, structure, jurisdiction, timing and execution parameters.

Compliance Readiness

Willingness to provide appropriate corporate, KYC and transaction documentation when required.

Direct Access

Reasonable access to the actual decision-maker, authorized principal or transaction-controlling party.

Engagement Process

Qualification precedes disclosure.

01 — Counterparty Registration

Provide essential information about your organization, authority, commercial requirement or sell-side position, relevant market and transaction capability.

02 — dandy Advisory Review

The submission is assessed for authority, credibility, commercial logic, counterparty fit and engagement suitability.

03 — Controlled Counterparty Path

Where a credible match or appropriate pathway exists, engagement may proceed through controlled disclosure and the relevant confidentiality, protection or transaction framework.

04 — Direct Engagement

Qualified parties may progress to principal-level or mandate-level engagement where the opportunity, authority and commercial parameters warrant it.

Submission does not guarantee access to any opportunity, counterparty, mandate or representation engagement.

Operating Standards

Relationships and information are treated as commercial assets.

Authority Before Circulation
Opportunities are not distributed without a sufficient basis for representation and a clear understanding of the relevant principal or mandate.

Controlled Disclosure
Sensitive information is released according to stage, relevance and commercial need.

Relationship Protection
Introduced relationships and originating interests are protected through appropriate commercial arrangements.

Engagement Fit

Selectivity protects everyone involved.

This channel is not intended for:

  • Anonymous transaction submissions
  • Unverified commodity offers
  • Requests for buyer, seller or investor lists
  • Speculative sourcing enquiries without a defined commercial requirement
  • Parties unable to establish authority
  • Mass circulation of confidential files
  • Unsolicited financial instruments, wallet evidence, proof-of-funds material or transaction screenshots

dandy Advisory does not operate as an unrestricted clearinghouse for commercial opportunities, instruments or confidential transaction material.

Complex opportunities require more than access.

dandy Advisory undertakes selected commercial assignments where structure, authority, representation and counterparty positioning are central to advancing a transaction.

Commercial Enquiries

Establish the commercial basis before seeking access.

In your submission, state whether you are a buyer or end-user, titleholder or seller, authorized mandate, or enquiring about a private structured situation.

Please do not submit confidential transaction files, KYC packages, instruments, proof-of-funds material, wallet evidence or transaction screenshots at the initial stage.

dandy Advisory will review the information provided and determine whether a relevant commercial or counterparty path may exist.
